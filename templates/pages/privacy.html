Privacy policy

Last updated: {% now "F j, Y" %}

1. Who we are

Resuma.pk ("we", "our", "us") operates the job search engine at www.resuma.pk. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and the rights you have.

2. What we collect

  • · Account data — name, email address, phone number and password (stored hashed).
  • · Profile data — headline, city, skills, summary and resume you choose to upload.
  • · Application data — jobs you apply to, cover letters and correspondence status.
  • · Usage data — pages visited and search terms, used in aggregate to improve the service.

3. How we use your data

  • · To operate your account and deliver our job matching service.
  • · To transmit your applications (including your resume) to the employer you applied to.
  • · To send service notifications such as application confirmations.
  • · To improve performance, security and relevance of search results.

4. What we never do

  • · We never sell or rent your personal data.
  • · We never publish your resume publicly.
  • · We never send unsolicited marketing without your consent.

5. Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You may delete your account at any time by contacting hello@resuma.pk; we will remove your personal data within 30 days except where we are legally required to retain it.

6. Cookies and analytics

We use strictly necessary cookies for authentication and security. Anonymous, aggregate analytics may be used to understand how the site performs. We do not use cross-site advertising trackers.

7. Security

We protect your data with industry-standard measures: HTTPS everywhere, hashed passwords, CSRF protection against forged requests, and restricted access to personal data within our team.

8. Your rights

You may access, correct, export or delete your personal data at any time. To exercise any of these rights, email hello@resuma.pk and we will respond within 30 days.
