Resuma.pk exists for one reason: job hunting in Pakistan should be as good as the world's best. No clutter, no outdated listings, no dead ends.

Why we built Resuma

Pakistan has over 60 million people under 30 — one of the youngest workforces on earth — yet finding a good job often means scrolling through cluttered portals, stale listings, and dead email links.

We started Resuma.pk with a simple idea: a job search engine that treats candidates with respect. Fast search. Honest job postings. Transparent applications. One clean place where talent and opportunity meet.

From fresh graduates in Lahore to senior engineers in Karachi, from remote-first startups to multinationals — Resuma is where Pakistan's careers begin.
